Make an editable lyric video from a finished song.

01 / PREPARE

Start with useful references.

A finished audio track

Use your own recording or a track you are authorized to process. This workflow turns existing audio into a lyric video; it does not generate a new song.

Lyrics and visual assets

Keep the intended lyrics handy for corrections. Only use cover art, photographs and background assets you own or are permitted to publish.

02 / WORKFLOW

Make one candidate. Then inspect it.

  1. Open the creator, sign in as required and upload the finished audio track.
  2. Review the proposed lyrics and word-level synchronization. Correct names, repeated phrases and hard-to-hear words.
  3. Choose a visual style and layout for the destination screen.
  4. Watch the full browser preview at normal speed, including transitions and the beginning and end of the song.
  5. Check the current export option and render only after the preview is ready. Inspect the exported video before sharing it.
03 / REVIEW

Look beyond the first impression.

Words and timing

Listen while reading. Check line changes, repeated sections and words near musical transitions.

Readability

Review text size, contrast, line length and movement. A line that looks clear while paused may be difficult to read during playback.

Framing and rights

Check crops and safe areas for the chosen format. Confirm permission to distribute the music, lyrics and artwork.

Treat automatic synchronization as a starting point for review. Keep a human check between transcription and publication.

04 / ACCESS & SCOPE

Check the current product limits.

A browser preview is available before paid export. Confirm current project, credit and export rules on the official site before selecting a plan. Features and plan limits may change; the official product website is the current reference.
